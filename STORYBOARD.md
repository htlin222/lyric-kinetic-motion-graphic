# Storyboard — REVERSE JING-JING（逆晶晶體）

作者 Song by **Benson Hung** ([youtube.com/@hbshun](https://www.youtube.com/@hbshun)) · 152 BPM · 3:46 · 1920×1080 @ 30 fps.
Every word, and every 中文 character, is timed to its vocal attack in the waveform (see README → Sync).

## Visual concept
A tongue-in-cheek **"international elite" award-show / press conference**: gold leaf, glitter, a rotating wireframe globe, a breaking-news ticker, and dead-serious serif type.

The joke is the *language switch*, so English and 中文 get two opposite motion vocabularies:

| | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Font | Playfair Display Italic, ivory | Noto Serif CJK TC Black, gold |
| Entry | glides in: yaw 70°→0, y-drift, 3-frame motion trail, soft glow | **slams** 2.6×→1× in 6 frames, like a seal (印章) stamp |
| Impact | none, weightless | camera shake, white flash, chromatic split, red seal frame, gold ink spatter |
| Depth | flat | 12-layer 3D extrusion toward the vanishing point |

**REVERSE** sections use Anton. The letters arrive **right to left** and each one flips in from a mirror image (it's red while mirrored). Below them is a floor reflection, and behind them a wall of mirrored "REVERSE JING-JING" text. Each chorus opens with a **whole-frame mirror flip** and a VHS "◀◀ REWIND" tracking glitch.

## Signature shots
* **Intro**: the title flips in reversed letter by letter. On the downbeat at 0:08, a red 逆晶晶體 seal slams down.
* **"Till the question gets to 政治細節"**: the first stamp. This is where the visual rule is set up.
* **"You don't say 'residential power' / You say 民生用電"** (and the 節能 / 儲能 lines): the English line gets a red strikethrough the instant the 中文 lands.
* **"This is not 卡住 / This is strategy"**: a red ✕ draws itself across the stamped 中文.
* **"Cross-strait relations / Getting heavy in your head?"**: the words sag under their own weight.
* **"um, I would say…"**: a typewriter with a blinking cursor, as if the speaker stalled.
* **"Vocabulary getting dangerous?"**: chromatic glitch jitter.
* **"Oxford could never / Cambridge could try"**: a VS split screen under a laurel wreath.
* **雙語國際競爭力！！！ (climax)**: seven characters slam 4×→1× one by one, each hit with heavy shake and flash, over spinning gold rays and a confetti burst. The English gloss rises underneath.
* **"Got the resume / suit / stage / global image"**: a checklist where each red tick lands on the vocal.
* **"Maximum flexibility / Minimum hesitation"**: a scale gag (huge vs. tiny).
* **"ALL HAIL THE KING" / "Long live the king"**: a 3D gold crown drops in.
* **Outro**: International · Professional · Bilingual · Wayne style · 中文 stack one per beat → **Perfect** → end card.

## Audio-reactive layer (real waveform data)
* **Press:** a gold broadcast scope line on the horizon. The 3D video wall has 8 panels, and each panel swaps its graphic every 8 beats: live spectrum, vox scope, confidence level meter, 雙語指數 chart, globe, ON AIR, an EN⇄中 flip, and a reach map. On top of that:
  * a HEADLINE card that flips between English and 中文
  * Taipei / London / Boston clocks
  * a market strip (中文 ▲, ENGLISH ▼, HESITATION ▼99%)
  * the BREAKING ticker
* **Glam:** a radial spectrum ring around the lyric.
* **Chorus / finale:** a 3D spectrum ridgeline terrain, 28 history slices flying toward the camera, mirrored with bass at the centre.
* **Academy:** a mirrored level-meter waveform.
* **Alert:** a red oscilloscope.

## Chorus entry
The previous shot does a single eased 360° Y-spin with motion-blur ghosts. It cuts on the edge-on frame, and the chorus spins in **mirrored**, then lands front-facing exactly on the first "Re-" syllable. The extra "Reverse Jing Jing" chants at 1:44 and 2:49 have their own shots.

## Global camera
* Every shot runs a slow dolly-in (+1.8 %/s) with a rotational drift.
* The background camera flies forward through 3D particle and tunnel space. Its speed depends on the section: 260 u/s in verses, 900 u/s in choruses.
* The background zooms slightly with the bass envelope, and there's a beat-pulse glow.
* Film grain and a vignette sit on top. Choruses get letterbox bars.
